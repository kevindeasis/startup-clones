# Northwind Robotics glossary and onboarding
Author: Tom Becker (Head of Ops)

Term: Atlas = Northwind Robotics' warehouse picking arm product line.
Term: Beacon = the fleet monitoring and uptime reporting dashboard.
Term: Cobalt = the internal codename for the next-generation battery pack.
Term: Pilot = a 90-day paid or free trial deployment at a customer site.
Term: ARR = annual recurring revenue.

New hires should read the Q4 roadmap first. Weekly all-hands is on Mondays; Maya Chen presents pipeline and Raj Patel presents engineering.
