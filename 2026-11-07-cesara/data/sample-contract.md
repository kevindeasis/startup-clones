# Commercial PPO Participating Hospital Agreement

Between Blue Harbor Health Plan ("Payer") and Riverside Medical Center ("Provider"). Fictional sample agreement for demonstration only.

## Article 1 - Term

1.1 Effective Date. This Agreement is effective January 1, 2026 and continues for three years, through December 31, 2028, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Article 10.

## Article 2 - Inpatient Case Rates

2.1 DRG 470. Major joint replacement or reattachment of lower extremity (MS-DRG 470) shall be paid a case rate of $24,500 per discharge.

2.2 DRG 291. Heart failure and shock with MCC (MS-DRG 291) shall be paid a case rate of $11,200 per discharge.

2.3 DRG 871. Sepsis with MCC (MS-DRG 871) shall be paid a case rate of $14,800 per discharge.

## Article 3 - Stop-Loss

3.1 Stop-Loss. For any Inpatient claim for which billed charges exceed $100,000, Payer shall pay the applicable case rate plus 70% of billed charges above $100,000.

## Article 4 - Per Diem Services

4.1 Behavioral Health. Inpatient behavioral health services billed under revenue code 0124 shall be paid a per diem of $1,350 per covered day.

## Article 5 - Outpatient Fee Schedule

5.1 CPT 99213. Office or outpatient visit, established patient, low complexity, shall be paid $125 per unit.

5.2 CPT 99214. Office or outpatient visit, established patient, moderate complexity, shall be paid $185 per unit.

5.3 CPT 70450. CT head or brain without contrast shall be paid $410 per unit.

5.4 CPT 73721. MRI of any joint of lower extremity without contrast shall be paid $780 per unit.

5.5 CPT 45378. Diagnostic colonoscopy shall be paid $950 per unit.

5.6 CPT 29881. Knee arthroscopy with meniscectomy shall be paid $3,200 per unit.

## Article 6 - Carve-Outs

6.1 Implants. Implantable devices billed under revenue code 0278 are carved out of all other rates and shall be paid at 65% of billed charges.

## Article 7 - Rate Escalator

7.1 Rate Increase. All rates in Articles 2, 4 and 5 shall increase by 3% for dates of service on or after July 1, 2026.

## Article 8 - Default Payment

8.1 Default Rule. Covered services not otherwise listed in this Agreement shall be paid at 55% of billed charges.

## Article 9 - Claims Administration

9.1 Timely Filing. Provider shall submit claims within 90 days of the date of service.

9.2 Appeals. Provider may appeal any underpayment within 180 days of the remittance advice date.
