# Risk Factors
Our business depends on a limited number of suppliers for key components such as lidar sensors and motor controllers. A disruption in supply could delay shipments and harm results.
We face intense competition from established industrial automation companies and well funded startups. Customer concentration is a risk: our top five customers accounted for 38% of revenue in fiscal 2025.

# Seasonality
Our results are typically stronger in the second half of the year as customers deploy automation ahead of peak holiday logistics demand.
