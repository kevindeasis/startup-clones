# Full-Year 2026 Guidance
The company is raising its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to a range of $1.56 billion to $1.60 billion, versus the prior range of $1.50 billion to $1.55 billion. Full-year gross margin is expected to be between 45.5% and 46.5%.
Capital expenditures are expected to be approximately $70 million for the year. These forward-looking statements are subject to risks described in our annual report.
