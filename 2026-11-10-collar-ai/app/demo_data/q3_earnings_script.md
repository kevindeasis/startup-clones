# Financial Results
Third quarter revenue was $412.5 million, an increase of 18.4% year over year, driven by strong demand for our warehouse automation platform. Recurring software and services revenue grew 27% and now represents 31% of total revenue.
Gross margin was 46.2%, up 150 basis points from the prior year quarter, reflecting manufacturing efficiencies and a richer software mix. Operating income was $58.1 million.

# Balance Sheet and Capital
We ended the quarter with $640 million in cash and no outstanding borrowings. Backlog at quarter end was $1.3 billion, up 22% year over year. During the quarter we repurchased 1.2 million shares under our existing authorization.
