# Northwind Robotics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Northwind Robotics (NWR) today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Revenue of $412.5 million grew 18.4% year over year. Net income was $41.7 million, or $0.52 per diluted share.

# Highlights
The company shipped its 10,000th autonomous mobile robot and signed three new enterprise customers in logistics. Customer count reached 214. The company announced a new AI-based fleet orchestration product, Northwind Conductor, now generally available.
