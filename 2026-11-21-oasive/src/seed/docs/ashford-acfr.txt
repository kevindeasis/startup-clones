City of Ashford Falls, Ohio - Annual Comprehensive Financial Report
Prepared by the Department of Finance. The City has a population of about 61,000 and is the seat of Ashford County.

FISCAL YEAR 2022 - General fund and pension indicators
Total governmental fund balance: $41,200,000
Unassigned fund balance at year end was 18.4% of general fund expenditures
Pension funded ratio: 61%
Top ten taxpayers accounted for 23.9% of assessed valuation
