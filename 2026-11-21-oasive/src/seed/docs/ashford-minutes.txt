City of Ashford Falls - City Council Minutes
Meeting date: {{d-9}}
=== Page 1 ===
Ashford Falls City Council - Special Session
Present: Mayor Calloway, Council Members Ibarra, Whitfield, Novak, Adeyemi, Park; Finance Director L. Brandt.
The special session was called to receive the report of the State Auditor.
=== Page 2 ===
Fiscal condition
The State Auditor declared a fiscal emergency and the City was placed under state fiscal oversight, with a financial planning and supervision commission appointed to approve the City's budget.
The Finance Director reported a general fund deficit of $14.2 million for the current year.
Council discussed the commission's request that the City consider layoffs and a hiring freeze.
