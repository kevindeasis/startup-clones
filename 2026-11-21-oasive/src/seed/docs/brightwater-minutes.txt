Finance Committee report
The Chief Financial Officer reported that the System obtained a waiver of the debt service coverage covenant from the bond trustee, covering $212 million of Series 2019 bonds, through the next testing date.
The trustees directed management to present a liquidity improvement plan within 60 days.
Mr. Hale resigned as Chief Financial Officer effective at the end of next month; a search firm has been engaged.
Management reported a year-to-date deficit of $9.4 million driven by contract labor.
