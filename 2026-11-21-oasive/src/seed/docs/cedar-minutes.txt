Administration
County Administrator Gwen Tallis announced her resignation effective at the end of the quarter.
The Board adopted the fiscal 2027 budget, which carries a projected deficit of $2.4 million to be covered from reserves.
The County Attorney reported a lawsuit with the City of Ashford Falls over shared 911 dispatch costs seeking $3.2 million.
