## Item 7. Harbor Street Lift Station Replacement - Design Services

Staff estimate total construction cost at $4.6M. Council voted 6-1 to authorize engineering services with Tidewell Engineering for 60% design of the Harbor Street Lift Station Replacement, not to exceed $410,000. The design will include a new wet well, two submersible pumps with variable frequency drives, a force main tie-in and a SCADA panel. Staff anticipate advertising for construction bids in spring 2027.

Councilmember Reyes asked about odor control. The Director of Public Utilities, Helena Marsh, said a carbon scrubber would be evaluated during design.
