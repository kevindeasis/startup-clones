# City of Brightwater
## Request for Proposals No. 2026-14 - Mill Creek Lift Station Rehabilitation

The City of Brightwater, Department of Public Utilities, invites sealed proposals from qualified contractors for the Mill Creek Lift Station Rehabilitation project. The engineer's estimate for the work is $1.85M. The project includes replacement of one submersible wastewater pump, new discharge check valves and gate valves, a variable frequency drive control panel with SCADA integration, and rehabilitation of the wet well.

Sealed bids are due December 18, 2026 at 2:00 PM Pacific at the Office of the City Clerk, 400 Water Street, Brightwater. A pre-bid meeting will be held December 2, 2026 at 10:00 AM at the Mill Creek Lift Station site. Written questions are due December 9, 2026 by 4:00 PM.

Late submissions will not be accepted.

## Section 2. Scope of Work

The Contractor shall furnish and install one submersible wastewater pump rated 450 GPM at 62 feet TDH, driven by a 25 HP motor with a variable frequency drive.

All swing check valves shall conform to AWWA C508 and be furnished with an external lever and spring.
Wetted components in contact with potable flush water shall be certified to NSF/ANSI 61.
Control panels shall carry UL 508A certification and include a PLC with SCADA communication to the City master system.
Ductile iron fittings shall conform to ASTM A536 Grade 65-45-12.

## Section 3. Bid Requirements

Each bid shall be accompanied by a bid bond in the amount of 5% of the total bid price, payable to the City of Brightwater.
The successful bidder shall furnish performance and payment bonds each equal to 100% of the contract price.
The bidder shall carry commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and name the City as additional insured.
The bidder shall have at least five years of experience supplying and commissioning submersible pump packages for municipal wastewater systems.
All welding shall be performed by welders certified under AWS D1.1.
Bidders shall submit one original and three copies of the proposal in a sealed envelope marked "RFP 2026-14".

## Section 4. Evaluation and Award

Proposals will be evaluated on price (60 points), qualifications and experience (25 points) and delivery schedule (15 points). The contract will be awarded to the responsive and responsible proposer with the highest total score.

Substantial completion is required within 150 calendar days of Notice to Proceed. The Contractor shall warrant all equipment against defects for twelve months from startup.
