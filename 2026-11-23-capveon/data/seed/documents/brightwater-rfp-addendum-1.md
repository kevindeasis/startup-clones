# City of Brightwater
## Addendum No. 1 - RFP 2026-14, Mill Creek Lift Station Rehabilitation

This addendum modifies the Request for Proposals and forms part of the contract documents. Bidders shall acknowledge receipt of this addendum on the proposal form.

1. The bid due date is extended from December 18, 2026 at 2:00 PM to January 8, 2027 at 2:00 PM.
2. The Section 2 valve specification is revised from AWWA C508 to AWWA C508 or C515 swing check valves.
3. A second submersible pump (Pump No. 2) is added to the base bid.
