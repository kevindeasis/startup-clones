# Cascadia Department of Transportation
## Invitation for Bids No. R9-2026-07 - Route 9 Resurfacing Phase 2

CDOT invites sealed bids for the Route 9 Resurfacing Phase 2 project, consisting of cold milling and placement of 3.5 inches of hot-mix asphalt pavement over 11.2 miles, plus guardrail and pavement markings. The engineer's estimate is $4.2M.

Sealed bids are due January 14, 2027 at 11:00 AM at the CDOT Contracts Office, 1200 Capitol Way. A pre-bid meeting will be held December 15, 2026. Written questions are due January 5, 2027.

--- page 2 ---

## Bidding Requirements

Each bid shall be accompanied by a bid bond of 10% of the bid amount.
The Contractor shall provide hot-mix asphalt conforming to ASTM D3515 and hold a current Cascadia prequalification certificate.

--- page 3 ---

## Award

The contract will be awarded to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder. Work must be completed by October 31, 2027.
