# Copperline County Board of Commissioners - Public Works Committee
August 19, 2026, 9:00 AM, County Administration Building

Commissioners present: Chair Dolores Vance, Commissioners Pruitt, Yamamoto and Achebe.

## Consent Agenda

The consent agenda, including the July 15 minutes and routine travel requests, was approved unanimously.

--- page 2 ---

## Item 4. County Road 12 Resurfacing Program

The Board approved funding of $3.1M for the County Road 12 resurfacing program, covering 8.4 miles of pavement between Fairmont Junction and the Copperline River bridge. Staff expect to advertise for bids in spring 2027, with construction to follow in summer 2027.

Commissioner Pruitt asked that school bus routes be protected during paving. Director Delacroix confirmed this would be written into the contract.

## Item 5. Fleet Replacement

The Board approved the purchase of four light-duty trucks under the state cooperative contract.
