# Copperline County - FY2027 Adopted Budget, Public Works (Excerpt)

The FY2027 adopted budget for Public Works totals $18.2M. Capital line items are summarized below.

## Hollis Creek Culvert Replacement

The adopted budget appropriates $950,000 in FY2027 for the Hollis Creek Culvert Replacement, including engineering and construction of two box culverts on Hollis Creek Road.

--- page 2 ---

## Rural Booster Pump Replacement

The budget appropriates $480,000 for replacement of the booster pump at the Copperline Rural Water System tank site.

## County Road 12 Resurfacing Program

The budget appropriates $3.1M for the County Road 12 resurfacing program, funded by the road fund and a state grant.
