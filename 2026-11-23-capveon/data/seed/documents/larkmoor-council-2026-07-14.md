# Town of Larkmoor - Regular Meeting of the Town Council
July 14, 2026, 7:00 PM, Council Chambers

Present: Mayor Ellen Tarrant, Councilmembers Boyd, Okafor, Lindqvist and Reyes. Absent: none.

## Call to Order and Approval of Minutes

The Mayor called the meeting to order at 7:02 PM. The minutes of the June 9 regular meeting were approved 5-0.

## Public Comment

Two residents spoke about summer parking near the library. No action was taken.

## Item 5. Riverside Pump Station Upgrade (Discussion)

Public Works Director Hal Brennan presented a condition assessment of the Riverside Pump Station, which serves the potable water booster zone on the east side of town. Staff reported that two of the three pumps are beyond their service life and the electrical controls are obsolete. Staff discussed an upgrade project with a preliminary estimate of $1.2M and asked the Council to consider whether to carry it into next year's capital plan.

Councilmember Okafor asked whether the work could include remote monitoring. Mr. Brennan said SCADA telemetry would be part of any scope. Council directed staff to return with funding options in the fall; no vote was taken.

## Item 6. Park Maintenance Contract

Council approved renewal of the park maintenance contract with Greenway Services for $48,000.
